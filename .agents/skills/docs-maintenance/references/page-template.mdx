## The idea: money on a ledger

The server's economy is a **ledger**: a permanent, ordered record of Credits
moving between accounts. A coin is not an item you carry; it is a line in that
record saying an account holds some amount. Because the ledger is the ground
truth, your balance survives restarts and every payout is recorded.

No commands yet — this section only has to leave the reader with the concept.

## The basics: what you need on day one

Credits are the server's currency, shown with the symbol ¢ and two decimal
places. Your balance is the amount you can spend right now.

Assume no prior knowledge here. Everything above the commands must make sense
to someone who has never heard of the feature.

## Everyday: what you can do

Two commands cover most of what you need. `/mint balance` shows your available
Credits, and `/mint pay <player> <amount> [reason]` sends Credits to another
player, with the optional reason showing up in the receipt you both see.

Commands live inside sentences that say when and why to reach for them. Most
readers stop at this level, so it must stand on its own.

## Advanced: transactions, holds, and virtual accounts

A transaction is the formal unit the ledger records: a debit, a matching
credit, and a receipt. Other plugins reserve funds with a hold before a trade
settles, and some accounts belong to systems rather than players.

Be honest about which parts a player drives and which are machinery running on
their behalf. Never let this material drift up into the basics.

## What it means for you

Balances are authoritative, so a payout that does not appear in
`/mint history` did not happen — ask an operator rather than paying twice.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
